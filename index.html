How it works

Three steps. About sixty seconds. The whole transaction lives between you, the creator, and the advertiser — nobody else.

01 — Watch

The advertiser fills your tip jar.

Find a video, hit play. A 30-second sponsor ad runs first. The moment it ends, the advertiser drops their bid into your tip jar — minimum $0.50, often $1 to $3 for premium audiences. Not yet yours to keep, but waiting on your next move.

02 — Tip or jip

Drop it in their jar — or in yours.

Tip if the video taught you something — drop your coin in the creator's jar with a thanks. Jip if it didn't quite land — keep the coin in your own jar with feedback that helps them sharpen the next one.

03 — Cash out

Your jar fills up. Empty it whenever.

Coins from tipped and jipped videos both add to your TipJip jar. At $20, transfer to your bank or PayPal — no fees, no thresholds, no waiting period. Or let it ride and tip the next creator.

How we get paid

We charge advertisers,
not you. Not creators.

Plain numbers. No hidden cuts. The same fee schedule for the smallest indie advertiser as for the biggest brand.

TipJip earns one thing only: a matched delivery fee from advertisers, added at the advertiser's checkout — never out of your jar, never out of the creator's tip. Viewers and creators only ever see the bid amount the advertiser chose, and they keep 100% of it.

Advertiser's bid → paid in full to the viewer
Matched delivery + 50% of bid · min $0.10 · max $0.50
Advertiser pays = bid plus matched delivery

The cap means premium advertisers pay a smaller effective rate as their bid grows — encouraging higher bids and richer audiences without runaway fees.
